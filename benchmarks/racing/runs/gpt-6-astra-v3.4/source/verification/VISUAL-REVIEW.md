# Inspected visual comparisons

The comparison sheets in `compare/` were opened and visually inspected. The left panel is the actual prototype screenshot; remaining panels are the supplied references, resized to the same dimensions solely for comparison. No reference pixels appear in the game.

## S1 position — FAIL, prototype chase view vs references 01 and 02

1. The reference has dense palm silhouettes, roadside objects and a detailed coastal horizon. The prototype has bare heightfield terrain with no palms, beach, sea, foam or roadside props.
2. The prototype rear bumper, wing and exhaust have substantially simpler shapes and fewer visible surface details than the reference car. The glass has a broad blue reflection rather than the richer environmental reflections in the reference.
3. The reference has localized warm rim light, detailed contact shadowing and strong differences between rough ground and reflective bodywork. The prototype has a broad asphalt highlight and a simple long directional shadow, with much less material variation.
4. The reference conveys speed through world detail and motion blur. This evidence is stationary, with one car, no race HUD or AI pack, and no motion blur.

Veto assessment: empty roadside, repeated terrain pattern and absence of sea/foam fail the final-art veto. These were not hidden or treated as completed Z1 work. Although there is visible sky and a cast shadow, that does not establish P2 visual parity.

## S2 position — FAIL, prototype 3/4 front view vs reference 11

1. The reference car has complex panel transitions, vents, layered bumpers and finely resolved wheel arches. The prototype front fascia is very simple and its trimmed wheel openings show uneven edges.
2. The reference paint and glass carry detailed reflections from the surroundings. The prototype hood has a broad pale highlight, the front end looks comparatively flat, and the glass lacks comparable reflected detail.
3. The reference tyre, wheel, brake and underbody treatment has richer surface variation and contact shading. The prototype spokes, tyre sidewalls and brake parts are present but visibly more repetitive and schematic.
4. The reference has textured architecture, vegetation and atmospheric light. The prototype surroundings are almost empty, with repeating terrain and a simple sky.

Veto assessment: the active car is not made of visible boxes, and it has curved geometry, separate materials, reflections and a contact shadow. Its numeric complexity is nevertheless insufficient evidence of commercial finish. The screenshot remains below P2's required car/material target.

## S12 position — bounded P1 topology evidence, no assigned reference

1. The full closed road loop can be identified; it has no missing segment in this overhead view.
2. Road/terrain depth precision at this high perspective-camera position causes mottled edge appearance. This screenshot is not a polished map presentation.
3. The coarse landscape pattern and rectangular outer terrain boundary remain visible. There are no completed art zones, sea, buildings or forest.

This top view is diagnostic evidence only. It is outside the S1–S9 image-sanity acceptance set, and it does not certify final environment quality.
